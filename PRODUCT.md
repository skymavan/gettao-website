# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Operations leaders and founders at growing small-to-medium businesses who are buried in repetitive operational work — triaging requests, reconciling data, chasing follow-ups, moving information between systems. They want to delegate that work to reliable autonomous systems, not drive a copilot, and they need to trust that every consequential action is observed, reversible, and approved by a person before it lands.

## Product Purpose

GetTAO — Get The Autonomous Operations — is a platform that runs business operations autonomously. Visitors must understand, within seconds, that GetTAO is a product (not a consultancy), that autonomous agents do the operational work inside guardrails the customer defines, and that a human approves every consequential action. The page must qualify fit through use cases, show the operating loop plainly, and turn interested operators into access requests.

## Brand Personality

Operational, alive, trustworthy. The brand should feel like a next-generation control room: autonomous systems running live, signals moving, a human hand on the throttle. Expansive intelligence made legible through operational discipline — never a black box.

## Anti-references

- Do not clone SkyMavan's Celestial Instrument identity, copy, navy palette, or Instrument Serif typography.
- Do not use AI-purple/violet styling, glass across content surfaces, gradient text, fake dashboards with fabricated metrics, fake client logos, or testimonials.
- Do not default to beige editorial minimalism, terminal/mono cosplay, or generic SaaS-template card grids.
- Do not describe the platform as a chatbot, a copilot the user drives, or an experiment.
- Do not present fabricated performance numbers. Capabilities are described honestly, never inflated.

## Design Principles

1. Show the system running: visual effects must explain the autonomous operating loop — observe, reason, approve, act, learn — not decorate.
2. Make autonomy legible: cinematic moments coexist with plain copy, visible guardrails, and an obvious path to request access.
3. Earn trust through control: human approval gates, observability, and reversibility are the proof, not unverified claims.
4. Build for the whole journey: the site serves people, search engines, AI agents, keyboard users, and reduced-motion visitors.
5. Performance is part of the brand: the experience feels immediate, on any device, whether motion is available or not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All text and interactive controls meet contrast requirements, navigation and forms work by keyboard and screen reader, focus stays visible, and all motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion`. Mobile and reduced-motion experiences are first-class, not afterthoughts.
