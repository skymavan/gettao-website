# GetTAO Content Roadmap

The launch site is intentionally one page. Future content should expand topical authority only when GetTAO can publish useful, experience-backed material rather than thin keyword pages.

## Pillar 1: Autonomous operations

- **What is autonomous operations?** — searchable awareness guide defining autonomous agents, guardrails, approvals, observability, and the difference between autonomy and a copilot.
- **Autonomous agents vs workflow automation** — searchable consideration article comparing when autonomous reasoning is useful and when deterministic automation is safer.

## Pillar 2: Human-in-the-loop and control

- **How to design human approval workflows for AI agents** — searchable implementation guide with a concrete approval-gate and confidence-threshold architecture.
- **What makes autonomous systems trustworthy** — searchable guide covering observability, audit trails, reversibility, and escalation.

## Pillar 3: Building and operating agentic systems

- **Agentic operations platform vs building in-house** — searchable consideration guide for teams deciding between a platform and internal tooling.
- **Connecting AI agents to your existing tools** — searchable technical guide covering integrations, data handling, rate limits, and error handling.

Every article should include a named author, visible update date, direct answer introduction, original diagrams or examples, cited primary sources, and links back to the relevant capability and pricing section.

## Post-launch search setup

After the production domain is live:

1. Verify `https://gettao.io/` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Submit `https://gettao.io/sitemap.xml` to both services.
3. Record impressions, average position, clicks, and AI-answer citations monthly for the commercial query set below.
